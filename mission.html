The mission

The people with the most to gain are being taught the least.

Gateway exists to close that gap — starting with anyone willing to spend ten minutes learning to ask better.

The gap

AI is the biggest change to how ordinary work gets done in a generation. The people best placed to use it already work in tech — they have the vocabulary, the confidence and the colleagues to ask.

Everyone else gets told to “just try it.” They type a question, get a generic answer, quietly decide it isn’t for them, and go back to doing the work the long way. That isn’t a talent gap. It’s a teaching gap.

Meanwhile the same tools are reshaping who gets hired, who gets promoted and whose job survives the next five years. A gap in good teaching becomes a gap in income.

Nobody should lose out on this because the instructions were written for somebody else.
Three rules

What we actually do about it.

Not a manifesto — three rules that decide what gets published here.

I.

The foundation stays free

The Starter Kit, the daily content, the prompts and the tool guides. Not a trial, not a teaser with the good part removed.

II.

Plain English, always

If something needs jargon to sound impressive, it gets rewritten. Anyone who can use a phone can use what’s taught here.

III.

Teach the skill, not the dependency

Even the paid programs end with you able to build the thing yourself. The goal is that you stop needing us.

No gatekeeping, in practice

It’s easy to say. Here’s what it costs us to mean it.

Full prompts, every time. No “message me for the full version.” If a technique is taught here, it’s taught completely.

Honest tool comparisons. Including when the free plan beats the paid one, and when the answer is “you don’t need to pay for anything yet.” There are no affiliate deals with any AI company, so nothing pulls the recommendations.

Uncertainty gets labeled. Pricing changes monthly, platform rules change without warning, and models gain abilities they didn’t have last quarter. When something is unverified, it says so.

Where this goes

A specific direction.

Right now Gateway is one person, a daily publishing habit and a free kit. Here’s where it’s pointed.

Now

Teach the basics in public, daily

Build the free library until there’s a real answer for whatever someone is stuck on.

Next

Go deep on the moments that change income

Getting hired first — then side income, everyday work, career changes and students.

In time

Reach the people who never see this at all

Workforce programs, community organizations, adult learners and people retraining after a career ends. It’s the long-term aim — and why the free tier isn’t a marketing tactic.
