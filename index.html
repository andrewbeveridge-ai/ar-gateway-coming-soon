What the formula gets you
Act as a hiring manager who reads 40 of these a week. Context: I interviewed Tuesday for an office coordinator role at a dental practice. The office manager mentioned they're drowning in scheduling errors since two people left, and I told her about the intake system I rebuilt at my last job. Task: write a short follow-up that reminds her of that specific conversation and makes her picture me solving it. Format: under 120 words, warm and direct, no clichés, no "I am writing to express."

What comes back: an email about her scheduling problem and your fix for it — the kind a hiring manager actually answers.
